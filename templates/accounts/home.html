System Stakeholders & Workflows

Designed to give each stakeholder explicit visibility into the recruitment lifecycle.

Students

Maintain a verified technical skill profile. Upload resumes for automated competency extraction and view detailed gap analysis against live industry roles.

  • Weighted skill mapping & proficiency levels
  • Automated resume keyword extraction
  • Personalized internship recommendations
  • Application stage & interview tracking
Industry Recruiters

Publish internship and placement openings with explicit skill expectations, minimum proficiency levels, and academic criteria.

  • Role postings with required skill weights
  • Applicant pipeline ranked by match percentage
  • Interview scheduling with calendar notes
  • Direct status updates to candidates
Faculty & Administration

Institutional oversight into student readiness, batch placement percentages, and market skill shortages to inform curriculum improvements.

  • Batch-wide skill supply vs demand charts
  • Department placement funnel tracking
  • Talent pool search by CGPA and skill
  • Historical placement and package data
